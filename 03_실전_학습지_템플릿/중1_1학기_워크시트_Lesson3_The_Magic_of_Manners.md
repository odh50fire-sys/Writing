## [정답 및 채점 가이드]
* **Step 1**: 1. visited / 2. helped / 3. went / 4. had / 5. made / 6. saw / 7. bought / 8. wrote / 9. didn't watch / 10. Did, meet
* **Step 2**:
  1. `I gave my seat to an elderly woman on the bus yesterday.`
  2. `Did you visit your uncle's house last weekend? No, I didn't.`
* **Step 3**:
  * ⓒ `didn't walk` (조동사 didn't 뒤에는 동사원형 walk가 와야 함)
  * ⓓ `took` (문맥 전체가 과거 시제이며 arrived와 병렬 구조이므로 take의 과거형 took을 써야 함)
* **Step 4 모범 답안**:
  `Last Sunday, I helped my neighbor carry heavy grocery bags up the stairs. She was an elderly lady and looked very tired. I didn't hesitate to give her a helping hand. She thanked me warmly with a bright smile. I felt really proud and happy all day long.`
