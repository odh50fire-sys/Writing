## [Step 1] 기초 문형 다지기 (Warm-up Drilling)
다음 빈칸에 `There is` 또는 `There are` 중 알맞은 것을 쓰고, 괄호 안의 동사를 알맞은 형태로 바꾸시오.

1. __________ a big mirror in the bathroom.
2. __________ two comfortable armchairs in the living room.
3. My dream is __________ (become) an interior designer.
4. I want __________ (decorate) my room with warm lights.
5. She hopes __________ (visit) London during the vacation.

## [정답 및 채점 가이드]
* **Step 1**: 1. There is / 2. There are / 3. to become / 4. to decorate / 5. to visit
* **Step 2**:
  1. `There is a small desk and a comfortable chair next to the bed.`
  2. `I want to play a new computer game with my friends this weekend.`
* **Step 3**:
  * ⓑ `to listen` (to부정사는 `to + 동사원형`이므로 listening을 listen으로 수정)
  * ⓓ `There are` (뒤에 오는 명사 `three small posters`가 복수이므로 is를 are로 수정)
* **Step 4 모범 답안**:
  `Welcome to my favorite space in my home. In my bedroom, there is a cozy bed near the sunny window. There are also many colorful comic books on the shelf. I like to read books and listen to music here every night. I hope to keep this room clean and peaceful.`
