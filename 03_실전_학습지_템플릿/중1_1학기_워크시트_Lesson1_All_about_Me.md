## [Step 1] 기초 문형 다지기 (Warm-up Drilling)

1. I __________ (be) a first-year student at Dongdo Middle School.
2. Minho and Jisu __________ (be) good tennis partners.
3. My older sister __________ (like) reading mystery books in her room.
4. He __________ (not, play) computer games on weekdays.
5. __________ you __________ (have) an English dictionary in your bag?

## [정답 및 채점 가이드]
* **Step 1**: 1. am / 2. are / 3. likes / 4. doesn't play / 5. Do, have
* **Step 2**:
  1. `Jinwoo gets up early and cleans his room every morning.` (gets up과 cleans 병렬 일치 필수)
  2. `Do you like to swim? Yes, I do.`
* **Step 3**:
  * ⓒ `cooks` (주어 he가 3인칭 단수이므로 동사 뒤에 -s 결합)
  * ⓓ `doesn't drink` (조동사 doesn't 뒤에는 동사원형 결합)
* **Step 4 모범 답안**:
  `Hello, everyone. My name is Minho, and I am thirteen years old. I am an active student who likes sports. My best friend Jiho always plays soccer with me after school. I want to make many wonderful memories in this class.`
