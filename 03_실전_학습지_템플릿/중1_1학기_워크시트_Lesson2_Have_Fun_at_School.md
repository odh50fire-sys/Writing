## [정답 및 채점 가이드]
* **Step 1**:
  1. `Listen to the teacher carefully!`
  2. `Don't run in the hallway!`
  3. `can play`
  4. `cannot swim` (can't swim)
  5. `Can I open the window?`
* **Step 2**:
  1. `Close the classroom door and keep quiet.`
  2. `Can you ride a skateboard? No, I can't, but I can ride a bicycle.`
* **Step 3**:
  * ⓐ `Join` (명령문은 동사원형으로 시작해야 함)
  * ⓑ `can learn` (조동사 can 뒤에는 반드시 동사원형 결합)
  * ⓒ `don't be` (부정 명령문 Don't 뒤에는 동사원형 be 결합)
* **Step 4 모범 답안**:
  `Welcome to Class 1-3! Here are our classroom rules. Be kind and polite to your classmates every day. Don't throw trash on the floor. Always clean your desk after class. Together, we can make our classroom a wonderful place!`
